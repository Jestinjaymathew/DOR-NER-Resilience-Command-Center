# DOR NER — Resilience Command Center

### From disruption detection to intelligent action.

**DOR NER** is an AI-assisted logistics and accessibility intelligence platform designed for the unique geographic, climatic, and connectivity challenges of India's North Eastern Region (NER).

It transforms road accessibility, weather conditions, vehicle movement, field incidents, and essential-supply information into actionable intelligence for disruption prediction, route optimization, supply prioritization, fleet coordination, and emergency response.

## 🚨 The Problem

The North Eastern Region faces major logistics challenges because of:

- Difficult mountainous terrain
- Heavy rainfall and extreme weather
- Landslides and floods
- Road and bridge disruptions
- Limited connectivity to remote areas
- Delays in transportation of essential goods
- Fragmented information across different authorities
- Limited real-time visibility of logistics operations

A disruption on one road can affect vehicles, deliveries, supply levels, emergency response, and entire districts.

Traditional monitoring systems often show **what is happening**.

DOR NER is designed to help authorities understand:

> **What is happening → What will be affected → What should we do next?**

# 💡 Our Solution

DOR NER creates a unified intelligence layer connecting:

**Weather + Roads + GIS + Vehicles + Incidents + Supplies + Field Reports**

The platform continuously evaluates this information and converts it into operational recommendations.
